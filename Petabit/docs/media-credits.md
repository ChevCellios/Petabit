# Media credits

## Starlink modem sound

`wwwroot/sounds/starlink-modem-15s.mp3` is the first 15 seconds of **Dial up modem noises**, by William Termini, released into the public domain by its copyright holder.

- Source and permission: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dial_up_modem_noises.ogg
- Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/33/Dial_up_modem_noises.ogg
- Original SHA-1: `48fee84220f9ea8385500faa0b2d6fd749e28d40`
- Adaptation: trim at 15 seconds, mono MP3 encoding, final 150 ms fade to avoid an abrupt cut. Served locally; no third-party audio request is made by the page.

Playback starts from the manual Ping Starlink click, at 35% volume. The visitor can disable it. Closing the Starlink panel, hiding the tab or leaving the page stops playback. The clip plays once and never loops; it is decorative feedback rather than a measurement of network progress.
