# Media credits

## Starlink modem sound

`wwwroot/sounds/starlink-modem.mp3` is the complete recording (approximately 28.6 seconds) of **Dial up modem noises**, by William Termini, released into the public domain by its copyright holder.

- Source and permission: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dial_up_modem_noises.ogg
- Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/33/Dial_up_modem_noises.ogg
- Original SHA-1: `48fee84220f9ea8385500faa0b2d6fd749e28d40`
- Adaptation: full-length mono MP3 encoding, without trimming. Served locally; no third-party audio request is made by the page.

Playback starts from every manual Ping Starlink click, at 35% volume, with no sound toggle. Closing the Starlink panel, hiding the tab or leaving the page stops playback. The clip plays once and never loops; it is decorative feedback rather than a measurement of network progress. Ping ISS likewise plays its existing sound immediately on each manual click, with no opt-in. Neither sound plays on page load or automatic refresh.
